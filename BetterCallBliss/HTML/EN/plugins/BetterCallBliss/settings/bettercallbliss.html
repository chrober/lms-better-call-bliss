The difficult-transition percentile currently serves both as the direct-transition trigger and the maximum adjacent-percentile target for Automatic destination routing. The optimizer searches complete paths with the selected effort and reports whether the target was reached; Exact uses the same adjacent objective for its fixed count. Separate trigger and target controls are planned. Current BlissMixer scoring, context, and repeat settings plus the Last.fm settings below are captured when the action starts.

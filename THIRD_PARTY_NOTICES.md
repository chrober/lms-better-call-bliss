# Third-party notices

Better Call Bliss credits Craig Drummond's [Bliss Mixer](https://github.com/CDrummond/lms-blissmixer).
The plugin package includes the separately released [bliss-playlist-optimizer](https://github.com/chrober/bliss-playlist-optimizer).
The optimizer's dependencies and their individual licenses must be reviewed against the exact source and Cargo.lock used to build the bundled binary.
